# Capstone · Step 7 — Post-mortem

## Objective
Write an honest short review of the whole capstone.

## Requirements
- What worked, and the evidence for it.
- What failed, and why.
- Every major architecture decision: would you make it again?
- What you would build differently with what you know now.
- Final numbers: quality metrics, cost per user, latency, number of users.

## Done when
- Someone who never saw the project could learn from the post-mortem alone.
