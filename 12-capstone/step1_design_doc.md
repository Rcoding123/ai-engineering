# Capstone · Step 1 — Design doc

## Objective
Before writing code, write a one-page design doc for an AI product that solves
a real problem for real people.

## Requirements
- Problem: who has it, how they solve it today, why that is painful.
- Users: who will actually use it (you need at least 5 real people later).
- Where AI adds value, and where a non-AI solution would be better.
- Architecture: which techniques from Projects 01-11 you will combine (at least three).
- Risks: what can go wrong (quality, cost, safety, privacy) and how you will notice.
- Success metrics: numbers that define "it works", decided now, before building.

## Done when
- The doc fits on one page and someone else can understand what you are building and why.
