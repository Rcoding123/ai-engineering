# Capstone · Step 5 — Real users

## Objective
Put the product in front of at least 5 real users and learn what actually matters.

## Requirements
- At least 5 people use it for a real task, not a demo you run for them.
- Collect feedback in a structured way (what they tried, what worked, what failed, what they wanted).
- Combine feedback with your traces: find the requests behind the complaints.

## Done when
- A feedback summary lists the top 3 problems, each backed by traces or quotes.
