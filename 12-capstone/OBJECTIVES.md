# 12 — Capstone

**Build:** An end-to-end AI product of your own choosing that solves a real problem for real users.

## Skills you will learn
- Scoping an AI product: where AI adds value and where it does not
- Combining RAG, tools, agents and evals into one system
- Making architecture trade-offs between quality, cost, latency and safety
- Shipping, collecting user feedback and iterating

## Requirements
1. Write a one-page design doc first: problem, users, architecture, risks, success metrics.
2. Use at least three techniques from earlier projects.
3. An eval suite with defined success thresholds, run before every release.
4. Deployed with observability, guardrails and cost tracking.
5. Get at least 5 real users to try it and collect their feedback.
6. Ship at least one improvement driven by eval results or user feedback.
7. Write a short post-mortem: what worked, what failed, what you would change.

## Done when
- It is live, people have used it, and you can defend every architectural decision with evidence.
